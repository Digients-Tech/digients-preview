# Digients L4 Explorer

## Physical scene and theme

A research engineer and a prospective buyer sit together in a sunlit meeting room, examining footage on a laptop and occasionally projecting it. A quiet pale mineral canvas keeps long annotations readable; the video is the single naturally dark, vivid surface.

## Color strategy

Restrained. Cool chalk and ink neutrals, with a cobalt accent reserved for playback position, selection and primary actions. All colors use OKLCH. A muted jade may identify available pose media; it does not decorate inactive elements.

## Typography

One precise sans family with native fallbacks. Calm fixed rem hierarchy: 30–34px episode titles, 20px section titles, 15px body, 13px supporting controls, 12px uppercase overlines. Tabular numerals and a compact monospace face for timestamps. Annotation prose never becomes a wall wider than 72ch.

## Layout

A slim identity bar and a compact dataset heading sit above a workspace. A quiet left collection rail holds search, category filters and thumbnail rows. The main canvas pairs a large evidence player with the active action's meaning. A horizontal subtask timeline anchors the player. Detailed action list and scene memory use tabs or inline expansion, not nested cards. On smaller screens the collection becomes a collapsible browsing panel and the explanation follows the video.

## Interaction

Video playback drives active action and subtask. Clicking a timeline segment or action seeks the same video clock. Media mode switches preserve time and playback intent. Scene memory exposes only observations that have occurred by the current video time. Search/filter changes preserve a valid selection and expose an understandable empty state. Loading uses stable skeleton geometry.

## Surface and motion

Hairline dividers, purposeful whitespace, 8–12px corners on the player and compact controls, minimal shadow. No decorative charts, blur, gradients or accent side stripes. Hover/focus transitions last 150–200ms and only animate opacity, color or transforms. Honor prefers-reduced-motion.
